<section class="about-section" aria-labelledby="cyber-method">
  <h2 id="cyber-method">How I approach security work</h2>
  <ol>
    <li>Understand the environment, business context, and scope.</li>
    <li>Collect relevant evidence before drawing conclusions.</li>
    <li>Distinguish observed facts from assumptions and hypotheses.</li>
    <li>Document findings, impact, limitations, and recommended next steps.</li>
    <li>Retest or validate changes where evidence and authorization permit.</li>
  </ol>
