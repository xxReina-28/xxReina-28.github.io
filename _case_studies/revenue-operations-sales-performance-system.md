## Business scenario

A multi-market B2B company records substantial sales activity, but inconsistent CRM stages, neglected opportunity age, and optimism-led forecast categories prevent management from assessing the quarter reliably.

## Problem and constraints

The system must distinguish pipeline volume from usable pipeline, identify leakage and aging, and support management action without treating unsupported opportunity values as credible revenue.

## Diagnostic approach

The simulation connects lead, account, opportunity, activity, forecast, and outcome data. It examines funnel progression, structured loss reasons, data completeness, next-step hygiene, stage age, qualification evidence, and forecast support.

## Key findings

- Activity volume does not establish healthy progression.
- Open value lacks consistent evidence for forecast judgment.
- Stage definitions and exception rules are not governed consistently.
- Management needs segment and deal-quality views, not only headline coverage.

## Designed future state

A governed revenue operating system defines capture, qualification, validation, proposal, commitment, and learning stages. Evidence requirements determine whether work advances and how it contributes to the forecast.

## Governance and measures

Daily exceptions, weekly pipeline and forecast reviews, monthly funnel analysis, and quarterly calibration connect reporting to decisions. Proposed measures include conversion, stage age, next-step hygiene, qualification evidence, close-date stability, loss reasons, and forecast calibration.

## Proposed implementation roadmap

The 90-day sequence establishes definitions and baselines, cleans and controls CRM data, introduces operating reviews, and calibrates forecast assumptions. It does not claim commercial adoption or realized revenue impact.

## Artifacts and limitations

The PDF includes a revenue data model, funnel diagnostic, pipeline-quality framework, evidence-weighted forecast, segment view, operating cadence, and roadmap. All companies, figures, pipeline values, targets, diagnoses, and outcomes are synthetic.
